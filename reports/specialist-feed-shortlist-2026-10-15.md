# Specialist feed shortlist — 15 October 2026

Only sources with at least four substantially useful reader-text samples out of five are listed. No feeds have been added to the app.

Feeds were fetched with the current app’s actual HTTPS downloader and parsed with its feed parser. The five latest eligible entries were seeded into an isolated SQLite database, then tested through the real saved-state, saved-results and reader endpoints. Each successful reader response was requested again to verify cached reopening. These were API tests from the Mac, not browser UI tests on the Pi. Opening and ending extracted text were spot-checked; complete preservation of every article is not guaranteed.

All 75 shortlisted samples saved and appeared in Saved stories. All 74 successful extraction responses returned identical cached text on reopening.
